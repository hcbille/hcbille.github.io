About

I'm HC, a systems architect in Stockholm. For close to a decade I've built the parts of a company nobody notices when they work: logins, laptops, email, and the access rules that keep auditors happy.

I've run Google Workspace for 9,000 people at Klarna and led tenant consolidations through acquisitions, most recently moving 2,300 Flowbird users into Arrive's Google Workspace without anyone losing a day.

I'm drawn to places where IT is a strategic function, and more and more to customer- and solutions-facing engineering, where having sat on the customer side is the useful part.

Off the clock I write Swift and C++ for an open-source browser, and I live in a terminal with Solarized on.

Summary

Systems Architect who has designed, built and run Google Workspace, identity and endpoint platforms for up to 9,000 users at Klarna, Arrive, Instabee and Bonnier News, and led tenant consolidations through acquisitions. Now moving toward solutions, customer and partner engineering, bringing the customer-side view of migrations, security and adoption. Hands-on with Okta, Entra ID, Jamf and Intune, and ships production code in Swift, C# and C++ (Chromium).
