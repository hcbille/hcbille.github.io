Case study: EasyPark → Arrive

Rebrand of every account, live on stage · 4,500+ users · 35 countries

The problem

EasyPark Group was becoming Arrive, and the new name was secret until it was announced on stage. At the moment of that announcement, every person's email address, groups and shared mailboxes had to move from the group's historical brands to arrive.com, in Google Workspace, Slack, Okta and the HR system at once.

Constraints

  • Secret until the reveal, so no pilot groups and no warning to users. Everything was rehearsed by simulation instead.
  • Three weeks from start to go-live.
  • A very small in-house group, because of how sensitive the project was.
  • All 4,500+ users in one go, with no downtime.

What I did

  • Led the change for Google Workspace and Slack, and kept the overview of how Google, Slack, Okta and the HR system depended on each other.
  • Knew who had to be involved, and brought HR in early enough that the HR system could change in step with everything else.
  • Kept the Workspace primary domain as it was, since changing it would have touched things outside our control, and renamed every user account, group and shared mailbox to arrive.com instead.
  • Scripted the whole change, so it ran the same way in the simulations and on the day, with no manual cleanup afterwards.

Result

All 4,500+ users moved to arrive.com the moment the brand went live on stage. Nobody was logged out or lost a session, and every old address kept receiving mail.

What I'd tell a customer doing the same

Stressful, but very doable. When every system identifies people by email address, the plan is mostly knowing who owns each system and bringing them in early, then rehearsing until the day itself is just running the script.
